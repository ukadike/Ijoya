# Ìjóyà — Status

## Current State

The repository documents a project in **concept and maquette development**.

### Exists now

- project concept and research framing;
- residency-facing project language;
- material and environmental design direction;
- accessibility architecture;
- relationship to Small Systems Lab and sibling projects;
- concept drawings, studies, and videos where separately documented or added to this repository.

### Proposed / not yet completed

- six full-scale outdoor sculptural bodies;
- final structural engineering and weather testing;
- final soft-robotic or sensing implementation;
- final garden installation layout;
- completed visitor testing;
- completed disabled-participant accessibility validation;
- completed residency installation;
- institutional partnership or residency selection unless separately documented.

## Documentation Rule

Every future artifact should be labeled as one of:

- `concept`
- `maquette`
- `prototype`
- `tested prototype`
- `installation`
- `documented observation`

The repository should never allow a concept rendering to read as evidence of a completed sculpture or installation.
